📝 Interview Preparation Tips
🧠 Technical Round
✅Practice 2-3 DSA problems daily on LeetCode
✅Focus on Arrays, Strings, Trees, DP and Graphs
✅Learn time and space complexity for every solution
✅Always explain your approach before coding
🎤 HR Round
✅Prepare "Tell me about yourself" — keep it 2 mins
✅Use STAR method for behavioral questions
✅Research company before interview
📄 Resume Tips
✅Keep resume to 1 page only
✅Add GitHub, LinkedIn and project links
✅Quantify achievements — "Improved by 40%"
